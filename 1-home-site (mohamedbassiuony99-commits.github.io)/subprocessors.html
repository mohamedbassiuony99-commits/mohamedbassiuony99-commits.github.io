Sub-processors

Last updated: 28 October 2026

These are the companies that process personal data for My Pet Book, what each does and where. We use them only for the features described in the Privacy Policy. Clinics in Vet mode are told at least 14 days before we add one (DPA, section 3).

CompanyWhat forWhere the data is processedWhen
Supabase, Inc.Database, sign-in and server functions for the app and the websiteFrankfurt, Germany (EU), on Amazon Web ServicesAlways, when you use an online feature
Anthropic, PBCReads scanned photos (Claude), checks review text, helps find the cause of crashesUnited StatesWhen you scan, write a review, or the app crashes
Google LLCReads scanned photos (Gemini) when Claude is busy; Sign in with Google; Google Play billing; Play Integrity device check; sign-in code emails (Google Workspace)United States and other countriesWhen you use one of these features
Apple Inc.Sign in with Apple; App Store billing; App Attest device checkUnited States and other countriesOn iPhone, when you use one of these features
RevenueCat, Inc.Tells our server whether Plus is activeUnited StatesWhen you buy or restore Plus
PostHog, Inc.Phone crash details (no screens, no usage counts)Frankfurt, Germany (EU cloud)Only after you say yes to "Detailed crash reports"
650 Industries, Inc. (Expo)Delivers app updates; push notices to the admin’s own phoneUnited StatesWhen the app checks for an update
GitHub, Inc.Hosts this website (no app data)United StatesWhen you visit the website

Apps you open from My Pet Book yourself (WhatsApp, the phone dialer, Google Maps) are not our sub-processors: what you share there is between you and them.

Last change: 28 October 2026 (anonymous usage counts on by default with a switch to turn them off; PostHog only for detailed crash reports, with consent; added Apple App Attest and Google Play Integrity for the device check; dogs and cats only).
