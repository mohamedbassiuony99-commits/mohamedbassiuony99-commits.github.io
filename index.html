Questions

PriceIs My Pet Book free?

Yes. The health book, reminders, widgets, vets and 10 scans a month are free, with no ads. Plus is optional and adds more scans, a daily cloud backup and looks.

AdviceDoes it tell me what dose to give?

No. My Pet Book keeps records only. It gives no dosage or treatment advice. Always ask a vet before giving any medicine, vaccine or treatment.

PetsWhich pets does it support?

Cats and dogs, including the street cats and dogs you look after.

PrivacyWhere is my data?

On your phone. Only what a feature needs leaves it, like the photo of a page you scan or the pets you share with family. The app also counts which screens are used, anonymously, to find bugs; you can turn that off in Settings. You can export everything or delete your account in Settings.

LanguageIs it in Arabic?

Yes, in Egyptian Arabic and English. The app follows your phone’s language; you can change it for My Pet Book alone in the phone’s Settings.

VetsHow does a vet join?

In the app: Settings, Vet mode. Fill in the clinic details, then send your documents on WhatsApp. We approve every vet by hand.
