Everything your pet’s paper book does, and more

Scan the paper book

Photograph a page of the vaccine book or a medicine box. The app reads stickers, dates and the vet’s notes and lists every vaccine and the next dose. You check each line before it is saved. 10 free scans every month.

Never miss a dose

Gentle reminders before boosters, dewormers and flea and tick treatments. Never at night, at most three a day. Your pet’s mood on the home screen shows when something is late.

Vets you can check

Vets who joined My Pet Book and were checked by us, with their clinic on the map and the animals they treat. Call or WhatsApp from the app. Ratings come only from people who contacted them through the app.

Visits signed by the vet

Show a code at the clinic. The vet adds what they gave and signs with the clinic’s PIN, and it lands in your book as “Confirmed by Dr X”. Anyone with the check code can verify it.

For the whole family

Share a pet with your family so anyone can log a dose. Only the pets you choose are shared.

Always ready

An emergency card with allergies, medicines and the microchip number. The whole book as a PDF, Excel export and import, a weight chart, an adoption hand-over file, and an EU travel checklist with an English PDF.

Looks just like your pet

Pick ears, colours and markings, or start from a photo. Hats, glasses and collars. Pixel art or drawn. Cats and dogs.

Street friends

Keep track of the street cats and dogs you feed: meals, shots and neutering. Show them nearby so neighbours can help feed them.

Their little world on your home screen

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets on iPhone and iPad, and home-screen widgets on Android. Your pet lives in a tiny pixel world that turns from morning to night, wears a new outfit every day, tells you what is due, and runs from the syringe when a vaccine is due today. Tap the pet and it hops; tap “Given” and the dose is logged.

For vets

Register your clinic in the app to appear to pet owners near you, see today’s patients and those who are late, and sign visits with your clinic’s PIN. We check every vet on WhatsApp before listing. Listing needs My Pet Book Plus; vets who join in the first three months after launch get their first year free.

Free, with an optional Plus

Pets, the health book, reminders, widgets and vets are free, for as many pets as you like. Plus adds more AI scans every month, an automatic daily cloud backup, extra app icons and backgrounds, more outfits and your pet’s name in colour: 55 EGP a month or 520 EGP a year (the stores may show slightly different prices).

Private by design

Your pets’ book stays on your phone. No ads and no ad tracking. An account is only needed for family sharing, cloud backup, vet mode and Plus. Crash reports and anonymous screen counts help us fix problems, and you can turn the counts off.

Questions

Is My Pet Book free?

Yes. The health book, reminders, widgets, vets and 10 scans a month are free, with no ads. Plus is optional and adds cosmetics, more scans and a daily cloud backup.

Does it tell me what dose to give?

No. My Pet Book keeps records only. It gives no dosage or treatment advice and does not recommend vets outside the app. Always ask a vet before giving any medicine, vaccine or treatment.

Which pets does it support?

Cats and dogs, including street cats and dogs you look after.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Only what a feature needs leaves it, for example the photo of a page you scan, or the pets you choose to share with family. You can export everything or delete your account in Settings.

Is it in Arabic?

Yes, in Egyptian Arabic and English. The app follows your phone’s language; you can pick its language in the phone’s Settings, under My Pet Book.

How does a vet join?

In the app: Settings, Vet mode. Fill in the clinic details, then send your documents to us on WhatsApp. We approve every vet by hand.
