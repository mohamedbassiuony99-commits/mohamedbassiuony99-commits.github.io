Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are. My Pet Book (دفتر أليفي) is made by Mohamed Bassiuony in Alexandria, Egypt, who is the data controller and personally responsible for applying the Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151 of 2020). Contact: mohamedbassiuony99@gmail.com.

The short version. Your pets’ book lives on your phone. Information leaves your phone only when you use a feature that needs it, and only what that feature needs. Usage counts are sent only if you say yes. We do not sell data, show no ads, and use no advertising or cross-app tracking tools.

Not medical advice. My Pet Book keeps records. It does not decide doses, treatments or any medical procedure, and it is not a vet. Always ask a vet before you give your pet any medicine, vaccine or treatment.

1. What stays on your phone

Your pets, health records, photos, notes, weights, your name and your settings are stored on your phone. We cannot see them. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and their text is never sent to us.

3. Why we may use it (legal bases)

  • To give you the service you asked for (your account, family share, vet visits, Plus): the agreement between us.
  • Our legitimate interest in fixing and improving the app for the anonymous usage counts and error notes. They are on by default because they carry nothing that names you; you can object and turn them off at any time in Settings, Help improve the app, and counting stops on that phone at once.
  • Your consent for detailed crash reports (the screen picture and PostHog). Without a yes nothing is sent; you can take it back any time in Settings.
  • Keeping the service safe and working: crash reports, rate limits, refusing throwaway emails, the device check.
  • The law: store and tax records of purchases.

4. Who processes data for us, and where

The full list, with what each one does and where, is on the sub-processors page. Our database is in Frankfurt (EU). Some services (Anthropic, Google, Apple, RevenueCat, Expo, GitHub) process data in the United States or other countries. These transfers are made only for the features above, with providers bound to protect the data at a level not lower than Egyptian law, and with the licence or permit of Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Center where the law requires one.

5. Your rights

  • Know what we hold about you and get a copy: export everything from Settings, Your data, or email us.
  • Correct it: edit in the app, or email us.
  • Delete it: Settings, Delete my account, or the steps below.
  • Object to a use, or take back a consent, at any time (taking it back does not undo what was lawful before).
  • Sign out of every other phone at once, and remove phones you don’t know: Settings, Sign-in and security.
  • Complain to Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Center if you think we got it wrong. Please tell us first so we can fix it.

We answer requests within the time the law sets. We may ask you to confirm it is your account first.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings, Delete my account. This deletes your account and everything on our server tied to it: your profile, family shares, street friends you showed, reviews, phones, consent records and vet requests. What is stored only on your phone is removed when you delete the app. If you are a vet, confirmations you signed on pets’ records stay, without your account, so those records stay true for their owners.

Without the app: email mohamedbassiuony99@gmail.com from the email you signed in with, subject "Delete my account". We delete the account and reply to confirm.

7. Children

My Pet Book is for adults (18 and over). Someone younger may use it only with a parent or legal guardian who agrees for them. We do not knowingly collect a child’s data without that consent; if we learn of it, we delete it.

8. Security, and if something goes wrong

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Every table on our server has row level security, so people can reach only their own data; secret keys live only on the server, never in the app or this website. If a breach affects personal data, we tell Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Center within 72 hours of learning of it, and the people affected as the law requires.

9. Changes

When the app changes what it does with data, we update this page, its date and version, and ask you to agree again in the app.
